Flower Hill Central Corporation

Westwind is one of several sub-associations that make up the larger Flower Hill community. Flower Hill Central Corporation (FHCC) is the umbrella organization. It runs the shared pool and community center, and every Westwind homeowner is automatically a member.

How Westwind Fits In

Westwind handles its own architectural control (fences, exterior changes) and its own board and dues. Flower Hill Central Corporation handles community-wide amenities, including the pool, the community center, and shared grounds. Every Westwind homeowner is a member by default. There's nothing to "join" separately, but you do need to register for the FHCC resident portal to access pool passes, amenity reservations, and community-wide communications.

Knowing who does what

FHCC or Westwind: Who Do I Call?

Every Flower Hill homeowner belongs to two associations at once. Here's the actual split, straight from FHCC's own FAQ.

Call FHCC · 301-208-1314

The Master Association

  • HOA fees & payments
  • Changing your mailing address
  • Resale packages
  • Community Center rental
  • Pool passes & registration
  • Common-area landscaping & recreational facility problems
  • Snow removal on common areas
Call Westwind · See Board & Contacts

Your Sub-Association

  • Neighborhood complaints
  • Parking issues
  • Trash & bulk item problems on your street
  • Exterior modification / ACC requests
  • Pet complaints
  • Snow removal on your own walk (single-family homes clear their own front/side walks; the County can cite you if you don't)

Selling or Renting Your Home?

Selling: resale packages are obtained through HomeWiseDocs.com, not directly from FHCC or Westwind. Renting: Montgomery County requires a rental license for any leased home in an HOA (administered by the Department of Housing and Community Affairs). An unlicensed rental can mean a $500 fine. Both of these are separate from anything Westwind's ACC handles.

One more thing worth knowing: Westwind's own Declaration of Covenants incorporates FHCC's master Declaration by reference (Article II). That means as a Westwind homeowner, you're governed by both Westwind's documents and FHCC's master Declaration, not just one or the other.
