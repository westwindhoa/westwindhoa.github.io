Board Meetings

Westwind's Board of Directors meets monthly by video conference. Every homeowner is welcome to attend. Below are the steps to join, when we meet, and what to expect at the annual meeting.

Monthly meetings

Third Wednesday, 7:30 p.m.

Board meetings are held every month by video conference using Google Meet. Homeowners are welcome to attend from a computer, tablet, or smartphone.

Google Meet video call
meet.google.com/ody-iwgu-azb
Ask to join the meeting

This opens meet.google.com in a new tab. Click the blue "Ask to join" button when you arrive. The board will let you in.

Prefer to call in?

Dial 754-217-7800, then enter PIN 119 235 619 followed by #.

Every third Wednesday

7:30 p.m., monthly, all year

Computer or tablet

Just click the link. Nothing to install

Smartphone

Download the free Google Meet app first

Joining from a phone?

Two Minutes, Start to Finish

Google Meet works right in the browser on a computer or tablet. On a smartphone, iOS and Android both require the free Google Meet app the first time.

  1. 1Search "Google Meet" in the App Store or Google Play and install it.
  2. 2At meeting time, tap the link above or type the code ody-iwgu-azb into the app.
  3. 3Tap Ask to join. A board member will let you in.
  4. 4No app, no problem. Call 754-217-7800 and enter PIN 119235619 # instead.
Third Wednesday of March

Annual Meeting

Held electronically each year, immediately before that month's regular Board meeting, using the same Google Meet details above: meet.google.com/ody-iwgu-azb, or call 754-217-7800, PIN 119235619 #. As with monthly meetings, a smartphone will need the Google Meet app installed first.

If quorum isn't met

Reconvened Meeting: April

If a quorum of homeowners isn't present at the March annual meeting, Westwind will invoke Md. Code, Corporations & Associations § 5-206 and hold a reconvened meeting on the third Wednesday of April at 7:30 p.m., using the same Google Meet video conference. At that meeting, the homeowners present constitute the quorum.

Couldn't Make a Meeting?

Approved minutes from every Board meeting are posted on the Meeting Minutes page, so you can see what was discussed and decided.

Questions About a Meeting?

Reach the board through the contact page, or see who's who on the Board & Contacts page.
