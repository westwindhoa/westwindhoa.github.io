Flower Hill Dolphins Swim Team

The Flower Hill Dolphins are the neighborhood's summer swim team, open to Flower Hill and Westwind kids and run by parent volunteers. The team manages registration, meets, and communication through Swimtopia, not through the HOA, so the links below take you straight to the source.

Team Basics

Coaching Staff

Head & Assistant Coaches

Led by a Head Coach with an Assistant Coach supporting practices and meets. See the team site for current staff.

Coaches page →
Practice

Two Age Groups

Practices are typically split into a younger group (10 & under) and an older group (11+), on the Flower Hill Community pool deck.

League

MCSL

The Dolphins compete in the Montgomery County Swim League, with home meets at the Flower Hill Community pool.

Volunteer Requirement

Every Family Pitches In

Families are asked to work a set number of meets and snack shack shifts, or contribute food for concessions. It takes about 32 volunteers to run a single meet.

Snack Shack

The Team's Main Fundraiser

All families sign up for shifts through a SignUpGenius link posted on the team site each season.
