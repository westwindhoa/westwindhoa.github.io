Emergency numbers, utility contacts, schools, and Montgomery County services: the numbers residents actually reach for.

Emergencies

911 for police, fire, or medical emergencies. For non-emergency police, call the Montgomery County Police non-emergency line at 301-279-8000, or the 6th District Station (Gaithersburg) directly at 240-773-8300.
