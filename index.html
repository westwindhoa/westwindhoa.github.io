A Few Things That Make Flower Hill, Flower Hill

Longtime resident Sonya Barnette told the Washington Post that "the circle that encompasses the common area is exactly a mile," a favorite of Westwind residents and a draw for walkers from neighboring communities too. Nike Missile Park, one of two parks serving the area, sits on the site of a former Cold War–era Nike missile base. Flower Hill has also been home to NFL players Stefon and Trevon Diggs, who spent their teenage years here.

Source: The Washington Post's 2022 "Where We Live" feature on Flower Hill.

About Westwind

185 Homes, Built to Last Since 1985

Westwind is a sub-association within the larger Flower Hill community, governed by its own Declaration of Covenants, Bylaws, and Architectural Control Committee (ACC) guidelines. The board is entirely volunteer-run by neighbors who live here.

  • Self-managed, volunteer board
  • Governed under Maryland HOA law
  • Original architecture by Pettit & Griffin
  • Gardenia Way, Cape Jasmine Way, and a portion of Flower Hill Way
