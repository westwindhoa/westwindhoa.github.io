Street Parking

A car parked on the street, in front of a house, isn't a violation of anything. Here's what Westwind's governing documents actually say, so the board has one place to point to when this comes up.

The Short Version

The streets inside Westwind are county streets, not private property, and not owned by whichever house happens to be nearest. Under the Declaration, a private automobile is explicitly allowed to be parked there. Nobody needs permission to park a normal car on a public street, whether or not it's directly in front of your own house.

Straight from the Declaration

What's Actually Restricted

The Declaration doesn't say anything special about parking directly in front of a house. What it does restrict is specific vehicle types, not where a private automobile can park.

“No commercial truck, commercial bus, or other commercial vehicle of any kind, or any boat, boat trailer or recreational vehicle of any kind shall be permitted to be kept upon any portion of the Properties, except as approved by the Architectural Control Committee.” Declaration of Covenants, Conditions and Restrictions, on commercial vehicles and RVs
“No portion of the Properties shall be used for the repair of automobiles, trucks, motorcycles, vans or any other type of motor vehicle, nor shall any vehicles other than a private automobile be parked on any portion of the Properties. No unlicensed vehicle shall remain on the Properties for more than seventy-two (72) hours.” Declaration of Covenants, Conditions and Restrictions, on vehicle repair and unlicensed vehicles
In plain English

What This Means in Practice

A private automobile parked on the street Explicitly allowed, no approval needed
A commercial truck, bus, boat, boat trailer, or RV Needs ACC approval to be kept on the Properties
Repairing a vehicle on the street or in a driveway Not permitted anywhere on the Properties
An unlicensed vehicle sitting for more than 72 hours The Association may have it removed at the owner's expense
Confirmed by the ACC Guidelines

These Are County Streets

The 2012 ACC Guidelines confirm the streets themselves are public right-of-way, maintained under county authority, not private Westwind property:

“Per Montgomery County Code and HOA covenants, commercial vehicles are prohibited on your property (with the exception of your garage) and on county streets adjacent to homes or schools.” 2012 ACC Guidelines

Note this restriction, like the Declaration's, is specifically about commercial vehicles. It doesn't restrict where an ordinary personal car can park on a public street.

Got a complaint?

What Actually Needs a Report

Not a violation

A Neighbor's Car on the Street

Even if it's not directly in front of their own house. Public streets aren't assigned parking.

Worth reporting

A Commercial Vehicle, Boat, or RV

Kept on a driveway, lawn, or street without ACC approval on file.

Worth reporting

A Car That Hasn't Moved in Weeks

Possibly unlicensed and past the 72-hour limit; worth flagging to the board.

To report a commercial vehicle, RV, or long-abandoned car, use the Report a Violation page.
