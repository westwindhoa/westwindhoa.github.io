Documents & Forms

Governing documents, board records, and the forms you'll need as a Westwind homeowner. Everything here is a PDF you can view, download, or fill out on your phone or tablet.

What the ACC actually does

Three Jobs, One Committee

Before you sell

Resale Inspections

Reviews the exterior of homes going up for sale to confirm they're in compliance before closing.

Before you build

Property Improvement Review

Reviews every exterior modification request to make sure it fits community standards and won't clash with neighboring homes.

After the fact

Notices & Warnings

Issues a notice when something doesn't match the approved guidelines, with a chance to fix it before further action.

Planning an exterior change?

Exterior Modification Request

Any exterior modification, including paint color, fencing, additions, sheds, and landscaping beyond routine maintenance, requires ACC approval before work begins. Fill out the form and submit it online, or use the fillable PDF if you'd rather.

Attach photos, a sketch, a material sample, or your contractor's estimate right on the form. The ACC receives everything by email, and you get a confirmation with a reference number.

Westwind Homeowners Association Exterior Modification Request

    Up to 6 files: a site plan or sketch, photos of the current condition, material or color samples, or a contractor estimate. JPG, PNG, PDF, or Word. Large photos are shrunk automatically.

    Your request goes straight to the ACC, and you get a confirmation email with a reference number.

    Prefer a Fillable PDF Instead?

    Same form, as a two-page fillable PDF. Type into it on your phone, tablet, or computer, then email or print it yourself.

    Renting Out Your Home or Using a Property Manager?

    The board keeps a record of which homes are owner-occupied, rented, or professionally managed, along with current contact information for each. If that applies to you, register or update it on the Rental & Property Manager Registration page.

    Thinking About Removing a Tree?

    The board is not currently requiring approval to remove trees. You don't need this form for it. See the Trees page for the one rule that still applies and what Montgomery County requires.

    Repainting or Replacing Siding, Trim, or a Door?

    Color is a big part of what the board discusses, and it needs ACC approval like any other exterior change. The full approved palette will be posted on the Approved Colors page once it's finalized; until then, submit your color choice through this form and the ACC will confirm it.
