Approved Exterior Colors

Siding, trim, and door color is one of the most common topics the board discusses. This page will hold the approved palette once it's finalized, in one place, so residents can check before they buy paint or order siding.

This Page Is Being Finalized

The board is working through the approved color list. Once it's settled, the specific colors, brands, and swatches will be posted here. Until then, any exterior color change still needs to go through the standard Exterior Modification Form, and the ACC will confirm whether a proposed color is approved as part of that review.
